Q3 call takeaways (20 Oct 2026)

  • $100B ARR by December (reiterated)
    “We remain on track to deliver our previously stated goal of $100 billion in ARR by the end of this year, defined as annualized revenue based on the month of December.” (CFO prepared remarks) [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • Compute: 1.7 GW end-Q3 → ~2 GW end-Oct → 2.5–3 GW end-Q4
    “…on track to reach nearly 2 gigawatts of nameplate compute at the end of this month alone in October and have a clear line of sight… to nameplate compute in the range of 2.5-3 gigawatts just by the end of this quarter.” [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • Q4 capex $35–40B (Q3: $19.6B)
    “…we expect fourth quarter capital expenditures to now be in the range of $35 billion-$40 billion for this quarter.” [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • Q4 gross margin back toward Q2 level
    “We also expect total company gross margins in Q4 to be more consistent with the second quarter, given some one-time benefits in customer mix that we highlighted in Q3.” Connectivity Q3 GM of 55% included ~3pt one-time benefit (tariff refunds). [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • Q1 2027 revenue $33–37B
    “We now see total company revenues in the range of $33 billion-$37 billion for Q1 of 2027.” [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • 2027 compute & ARR (Musk)
    “I would be surprised if we brought on less than 13 gigawatts of compute by the end of next year.” Musk sees ARR of $500B–$1T by end-2027. [Investing.com, "Earnings call transcript: SpaceX tops Q3 2026 estimates" (20 Oct 2026)]
  • Compute pricing / backlog
    Pricing “slightly above the high end of the $30–$50 per watt range”, payback under a year; backlog $63B at 30 Sep, ~$42B to be recognised within a year. [Yahoo Finance, SpaceX earnings live updates (20 Oct 2026)]

Q4'26E assumptions

  1. Revenue: monthly revenue ramps linearly from the Q3 average month (14,575/3 = 4,858, treated as August) to December = $100B/12 = 8,333 → Oct 6,596, Nov 7,465, Dec 8,333; Q4E = 22,394.
  2. Space flat at Q3 ($1,767M); Connectivity +15% QoQ (after +32% and +22%); SI = remainder (driven by 1.7→2.5–3 GW and a ~$13B/yr contract billing from 1 Dec).
  3. Gross margin: consolidated 55.27% (Q2'26: 4,319/7,814); Space 65.8% and Connectivity 52.0% at Q2'26 margins; SI gross profit = plug (implied 55.4%).
  4. Adj. EBITDA: Space = average of Q2'26 and Q3'26 (76); Connectivity margin = Q3'26 (66.7%) less 3pt one-time = 63.7%; SI = 80% of SI gross profit (Q2'26: 79%, Q3'26: 101%). Segments sum to consolidated (as in reported quarters).
  5. Net income = Adj. EBITDA − D&A (5,284, Q3 +45% given $35–40B capex) − SBC (1,261, +10%) − acquisition SBC (1,917 = $23B Cursor awards / 12 quarters) − interest expense (743, +10%) + interest income (873, flat); other income 0; tax 0 (Q3 had a benefit); no restructuring.
  6. Diluted EPS = net income / 12,475M diluted shares (Q3'26 weighted average).

Opus (anthropic/claude-opus-5) independently recomputed the first-draft projection and found no arithmetic errors (all lines matched within $1M rounding; its bridge reproduced Q3'26 net income within $11M). It flagged two optimistic assumptions: SI EBITDA at ~90% of SI gross profit and D&A +30%. These were revised to 80% and +45%, lowering Q4'26E EBITDA from 11,194 to 10,381, net income from 3,410 to 2,050 and EPS from $0.27 to $0.16. Opus also noted a geometric monthly ramp would give ~$22.0B revenue, and SI revenue (+93% QoQ vs +47–76% capacity) may be $1–3B high. Opus's suggested range: revenue $21.5–22.5B, EBITDA $9.5–11.2B, EPS $0.12–0.27.
